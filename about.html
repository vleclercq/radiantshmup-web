Who I am

I'm a senior software and quantitative engineer with a background in financial markets, algorithmic trading and high-frequency systems, where architecture, data, latency and reliability decide everything. I also design and build mobile applications, from the first concept to the store release.

Radiant Shmup is one of them: a vertical arcade shooter written in Rust, where every boss is built from parts you can blow off one by one.

What the game is built around

  • Readable bullet patterns and tight touch controls.
  • Bosses you take apart piece by piece, each part revealing a weak point.
  • No ads. Levels 1–2 are free; one annual Pro subscription unlocks levels 3–10.
