RADIANT SHMUP

Retro bullet hell arcade shooter: 10 worlds, huge bosses, upgradable weapons.

Google Play — coming soon App Store — coming soon

Blast through a 10-world alien invasion in a retro-style bullet hell arcade shooter built for one-handed, pick-up-and-play sessions on your phone or tablet. Drag to dodge waves of enemy fire, chain kills into a blazing combo streak, and go toe-to-toe with screen-filling bosses at the end of every world.

Features

  • ★ 10 handcrafted worlds, each with its own terrain, weather and boss fight.
  • ★ 3 weapons to master — rapid-fire Vulcan cannon, homing missiles, and a charged energy beam.
  • ★ Kill combo system with a blazing "chain" bonus every 25 kills.
  • ★ Wingmen — escort fighters you can swing around your ship with the formation dial.
  • ★ Classic arcade power-ups: shields, bombs, rapid fire, extra lives, weapon upgrades.
  • ★ Campaign map & ranks — track your best score and rank (C through S) on every level.
