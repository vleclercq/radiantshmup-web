Questions, bug reports, or trouble with a purchase: email vleclercq@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Pro subscription issues

Pro is managed entirely through your Apple ID or Google Play account. To cancel, change, or check a renewal date, go to your account's subscription settings on the store you purchased through — the app itself doesn't process payments. If a purchase didn't unlock Pro, try "Restore Purchases" from the app's Pro screen first; if that doesn't fix it, email us with your purchase date and platform (iOS/Android).
